Legislative Barriers: 4 Commonwealth Acts

Four Commonwealth Acts mandate cheque payment exclusively and must be amended before the system can close:

Additionally: state gambling legislation must be amended; the Cheques Act 1986 will be repealed. The Treasury Laws Amendment (Modernising Business Communications) Act 2023 already repealed one prior ATO cheque mandate.

Risk Factors & Caveats

UK Precedent: The UK attempted to abolish cheques by 2018 and reversed course due to public opposition from elderly and charity groups. Similar political pressure could delay Australia's timeline.
Digital Exclusion: 66.5% of Australians 75+ are digitally excluded; 40.9% of First Nations people; ~1.1M with disability are "highly digitally excluded" (2025 ADII).
Legislative Reform Required: 4 Commonwealth Acts + state gambling laws must be amended. No confirmed timeline for all amendments.
Volume Still Material: Despite <0.1% of transactions, cheques still cleared ~$12.89B monthly (Feb 2025). 3.5M bank cheques worth $820M remain uncashed.
Charity Sector: Heart Foundation, Greenpeace, Sydney Eye Hospital Foundation have published guidance on cheque phase-out impact on donations.
Volume Heuristic Limitations: Market share estimates use deposit share as proxy. RBA C5/C5.1 data tables should be consulted for material financial decisions.

Sectors Still at Risk

Property Conveyancing

Bank cheques were 9% of volume but >50% of value. Largely migrating to PEXA digital settlement.

Government Payments

Older Australians

Charities & Regional SMEs

Key Findings

  1. Terminal decline, not yet dead. At <0.1% of retail payments and declining 20%+ annually, cheques are functionally irrelevant. But $12.89B/month still clears.
  2. Smaller banks exited first — driven by third-party processing agreements expiring, not customer demand.
  3. Big 4 are life support. CBA, Westpac, NAB, ANZ process ~80% of remaining cheques and guarantee participation until September 2029.
  4. CBA & NAB moved earliest (Mar–Jun 2023). Westpac moved last — still issuing personal cheque books as of early 2026.
  5. Stale cheque mechanism is elegant: 15-month gap between issuance and acceptance cessation means all valid cheques expire naturally.
  6. Legislative reform required: 4 Commonwealth Acts mandate cheque payment exclusively. Government is actively reforming (one ATO mandate already repealed).
